  1. Recap the project of this chapter. Responses to model match sounds distinguish models. Stimuli matched to the spectral temporal model replicate primary versus non-primary differences, as we saw in the Norman, Higner and McDermott 2018 experiment. In stimuli matched to standard trained networks evoke responses most dissimilar to those of natural sounds on average. While
  2. stimuli matched to robust network, networks evoke responses similar to those of natural sounds. What this tells us is that monomatch stimuli indicate greater brain similarity for the robust network than alternative benchmarks.   3. that when we are comparing models to the brain, we should be including model matched or model derived stimuli to evaluate the models.
